\section{Introduction}

The Brazilian Congress on Quantum Science and Technologies (CBCTQ 2026) will take place from September 21 to 25, 2026, at the Innovation District of Cantareira, Niterói--RJ, Brazil.

CBCTQ 2026 succeeds the Workshop-Escola de Computação e Informação Quântica (WECIQ), consolidating nearly two decades of scientific exchange in quantum technologies in Brazil. The Congress expands its scope to include interdisciplinary developments in quantum computing, communication, information theory, metrology, and technological innovation.

Full papers must not exceed five pages in A4 format, double column, 10pt font, including references.

\section{Example of Mathematical Content}

Papers should present rigorous theoretical developments, experimental results, or technological innovations in quantum science.
\subsection{Example from Quantum Computing}

Consider a quantum state in a Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}$:

\begin{equation}
    \ket{\psi} = \sum_{x \in \{0,1\}^n} \alpha_x \ket{x},
\end{equation}

where $\sum_x |\alpha_x|^2 = 1$.

A quantum operation is represented by a completely positive trace-preserving (CPTP) map:

\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{E}(\rho) = \sum_k E_k \rho E_k^\dagger,
\end{equation}

with $\sum_k E_k^\dagger E_k = I$.

\subsection{Example from Quantum Information}

The von Neumann entropy of a density operator $\rho$ is defined as

\begin{equation}
    S(\rho) = -\mathrm{Tr}(\rho \log \rho).
\end{equation}

In quantum key distribution, the asymptotic secret key rate under collective attacks can be written as

\begin{equation}
    r = H(A|E) - H(A|B),
\end{equation}

where $H(A|E)$ and $H(A|B)$ denote conditional entropies.

\subsection{Example from Quantum Metrology}

The quantum Cramér–Rao bound establishes that

\begin{equation}
    \Delta \theta \ge \frac{1}{\sqrt{\nu F_Q(\rho_\theta)}},
\end{equation}

where $F_Q$ is the quantum Fisher information and $\nu$ is the number of measurements.
